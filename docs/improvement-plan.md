# Real Role improvement plan (October 2026)

Written by Claude Code on 2026-10-15, at `main` commit `9fbd395` (Phase 4). This plan has four parts:

1. What is broken or missing, found by pressing every button.
2. How to fix it.
3. A UI redesign drawing on Clicky, Airbnb, Shopify, Spotify and Stripe, plus the Claude Code skills to use for it.
4. A ranked list of add-on features, based on what job seekers are asking for right now.

Ownership follows A7. Everything in Parts 1–3 is in `apps/web` or `apps/extension` (Claude Code), except the board parsers. Those belong to Codex and go through `docs/HANDOFF.md`.

### 3.2 Design principles, combining the five

1. **Show the evidence, then the number.** The score ring's segments are the signals, and they visibly add up to the score (contract invariant).
2. **One primary action per screen** (Shopify). Check, Save, Follow up.
3. **Calm by default, delightful at the moments that matter** (Airbnb, Spotify): the score reveal, saving, and the follow-up nudge. Everywhere else, no motion.
4. **Keyboard-first for power users** (Stripe): `/` search, `?` shortcuts, `j/k` rows, `Alt+R` panel.
5. **Neutral language everywhere.** Freshness bands, never verdicts.

### 3.3 Design system (tokens first)

- **Where:** a new `packages/ui-tokens` (or `apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css`, imported by the extension's `style.css`). Use CSS variables exposed through Tailwind v4 `@theme`.
- **Colour:** keep the green brand, add a warm accent for delight moments, and use a single freshness ramp (low → medium → high) with text colours computed to pass 4.5:1. Full **light and dark** sets, with `:root[data-theme]` overrides and a theme toggle in the header.
- **Type:** Inter for UI. Add a display face for headings (e.g. Inter Display or Geist), and use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for every score and date.
- **Shape and depth:** 12–16 px radii on cards, 999 px on pills. Two shadow levels only.
- **Motion:** 150–250 ms ease-out. A score count-up (600 ms) and staggered signal rows, all disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Use the View Transitions API for row → drawer and check → result.
- **Components (shadcn/ui on Radix):** Button, Input, Tabs, Dialog/Drawer, Toast (sonner), DropdownMenu, Command (⌘K), Tooltip, Badge, Table, Calendar (follow-up date). Shared `ScoreRing`, `FreshnessPill`, `SignalRow` and `EvidenceBadge` live in one place and are reused by web and extension.

### 3.4 Surface-by-surface

**Landing (`/`)**

- Hero: the headline "Is this job still hiring?" stays. Below it sit the CTAs **"Add to Chrome, free"** (primary) and "Check a posting" (secondary).
- On the right, a **live mini-checker** with three fictional example chips (Fresh, Reposted, Vague). Clicking a chip animates the ring and rows. This fixes B19.
- A trust strip: "Reads only the page you're viewing · No résumé · Official careers-page APIs."
- A problem section with 2–3 sourced stats (cite them; the brief's 69% needs a source, and Resume Genius 2026 reports 67%).
- A bento grid of the five signals. A side-by-side of the extension on a fictional posting. Privacy principles. An FAQ. A final CTA.

**Check (`/check`)**

- A single Airbnb-style pill input that auto-detects a URL or pasted text, with an "Add title/company" disclosure.
- The result appears in place with a count-up ring, evidence badges and "Show details" per signal.
- A sticky action bar: **Save & track** · Share result (a link to a public, fictional-safe result page) · Check another.

**Dashboard (`/dashboard`)**

- KPI row: active, due this week, average freshness, reply rate.
- Filter chips, saved views and search above the table.
- Table columns: role, freshness pill, status (coloured select), applied, follow-up (date picker, with a bell when due), last activity.
- Row click → **drawer** with posting snapshot, signals at save time, notes, a status timeline, "Mark followed up" and "Draft follow-up".
- Bulk select. Undo toast on remove. A kanban toggle (Saved → Applied → Interviewing → Offer) for people who prefer boards (Huntr and Teal users expect one).
- The empty state shows an illustration, "Track your first application", and two buttons: "Add to Chrome" and "Add manually".

**Sign in**

- A centred card with Email (magic link) as the primary option, plus Google and GitHub.
- One line on what's stored.
- After sign-in, the user returns to where they were (fixes B4).

**Extension badge**

- A pill anchored near the title when it can be found, with a fallback corner pill that the user can drag and that remembers its position.
- Colour from the band, with a tooltip "87 · High freshness · click for why".
- A "Tracked ✓" state.

**Extension side panel**

- Header: ring, band, confidence, and a "Tracked" chip.
- **Evidence badges** row.
- **Interactive signal rows**: expand for the explanation, plus "Show on page" (Clicky-style highlight).
- Sticky footer: **Save & track** / status select once tracked.
- Empty, error and not-a-posting states each get a next step (B13).
- Dark mode follows Chrome.

## 4. Add-on features by market demand

**How this was researched (2026-10-15).** Direct page fetches of Reddit, X, Instagram, LinkedIn, the Chrome Web Store and Hacker News were blocked from this environment. Evidence therefore comes from web-search results: Hacker News and Teamblind threads, press coverage of TikTok and Reddit trends, competitor Chrome Web Store listings (via aggregators), surveys, and legislation trackers. **There are no Reddit upvote counts or X threads below.** Treat the ratings as directional, and confirm them with a quick manual pass on r/jobs and r/recruitinghell before committing to a roadmap. Many surveys are vendor-run.

**Context:**

- Greenhouse classed 18–22% of jobs on its own platform as ghost jobs per quarter (2024; the method is disputed).
- 67% of US job seekers suspected an unfilled posting at least a few times (Resume Genius 2026).
- 63% say an employer ghosted them after an interview (Greenhouse 2025).
- Ontario has required an "existing vacancy" statement in postings since 2026-01-01. New York S8877 (hiring-timeline disclosure) passed both houses in June 2026. Similar bills are pending in New Jersey, California and Pennsylvania.
